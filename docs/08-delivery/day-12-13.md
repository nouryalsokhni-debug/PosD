### POSD-106 · Day 12 — Finish the Cashier Screens and Fix the Component Library
Labels `design`, `module-2`

**Context** — Day 10 (POSD-102) is done and Day 11 (POSD-103) is nearly done: four till screens in English and Arabic, every colour bound to a variable. Two things are left on Day 11, and the note *"For design — library gaps found while building"* lists fixes the front end needs before handoff. Components come before new screens.

**Design**
1. **Finish Day 11 (POSD-103)**
   - Till sizes: Sale and Payment at **1280 × 800 and 1366 × 768**, in English and Arabic (drawn at 1440 × 900). Options and Paid only if their layout changes.
   - Payment: the **wallet reference** state (Syriatel Cash / Sham Cash selected → reference field required) and the **offline** state (card and wallets disabled, "Needs connection").
2. **Component library**
   - Arabic variants inside the Button, Badge, Sidebar item and Owner tag sets (Lang=AR), with no detached main components.
   - Replace IBM Plex Sans Arabic (not installed) with the font of the Arabic text styles: top bar name, avatar, logo, Arabic buttons.
   - Move "brand" and "brand-soft" into the local colour collection, one value per layer (Quantara · Tenant HQ · Branch).
   - New components: text input, select, checkbox, radio, tabs, banner, dialog, meter, sync note, override tag. Add **radius-xs = 4** for checkboxes.
   - Owner tag: add a text property.
3. **Housekeeping**
   - Restore the **References** page (asked on Day 9).
   - Restore **01 Quantara · Shell** in English and Arabic.
   - Give a text style to the unstyled text layers on the Tenant HQ screens.

**Done when**
- Sale and Payment exist at both till sizes in English and Arabic, with the wallet-reference and offline states
- Every screen uses the new components; no detached Arabic main components; no text layer outside an instance without a text style
- The References page and the Quantara shell are back

**Not in this task:** new screens · prototype links · the "For the PO" questions (answered separately).

### POSD-107 · Day 13 — Cashier Part 2: Orders, Pay Later, Kitchen, Invoices, Offline and Power Cut
Labels `design`, `module-2`

**Context** — the backend builds Module 2 (Sale) next, and the front end needs the rest of the sale flow designed one step ahead. Reference: `prototype/pos.html` (titles and fields only) — Orders, Kitchen, Invoices, Menu → To print, and status pill → Demo for the offline and power-cut cases. Behaviour: `docs/03-flows/FLOW-02`, `FLOW-03`, `FLOW-05`.

**Design (till, 1366 × 768 first; English and Arabic)**
1. **Orders board** — Preparing / Ready / Handed over; pay-later state (unpaid tag, "Take payment"); "ticket not printed" tag
2. **Kitchen view** — cards by station, waiting time, late (red), new; station filter
3. **Invoices** — list with sync state; invoice record with the 80 mm receipt preview, Reprint (COPY), Cancel (manager PIN + reason), Refund cash
4. **States on the Sale screen** — offline for hours (red banner), new day with the last known rate, retention warning, back online with the sync-report dialog
5. **Power cut** — "payment restored" banner; "Was this payment received?" dialog
6. **To print** — failed print jobs, printer status, Retry / Retry all

**Done when**
- The six items exist in English and Arabic at 1366 × 768, built from the shared components (after Day 12)
- New field questions go into a "For the PO" note, as on Days 10–11

**Not in this task:** shift close · customer display · split bill · staff meal (Day 14).
